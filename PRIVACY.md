# Privacy policy

LapFold has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no telemetry.

**Accessibility permission.** LapFold uses it only to:
- type the keys you press into the text box you selected,
- tap, long-press and scroll at the position of its on-screen pointer,
- hide the on-screen keyboard while LapFold is on, and press Back / Home / split screen for you.

It does not read, record, store or send what is on your screen or what you type.

**Internet.** LapFold makes one request to `api.github.com/repos/ilukezippo/LapFold/releases/latest` when you open it (and when you tap *Check for updates*) to see if a newer version exists. Nothing about you or your device is sent.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
